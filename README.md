## About Me  

I am a data analyst who thrives on turning complexity into clarity. My work combines analytical thinking with structured execution, bridging the gap between raw data and decisions that matter. Skilled in Python, SQL, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI, I focus on creating solutions that are both insightful and practical.  

I believe that well designed analytics are not just about numbers they are about revealing patterns, improving outcomes, and enabling smarter choices. With curiosity and discipline as my framework, I aim to deliver work that is precise, purposeful, and impactful.
